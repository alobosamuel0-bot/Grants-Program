1️⃣ Project Overview

Objective: Build an AI-powered platform that helps businesses generate optimized product listings, marketing content, and operational guidelines. Integrates Web3 technology for decentralized data ownership and token-based incentives.

Problem: Small and medium businesses often fail to maximize sales because they lack tools to optimize product listings, marketing content, and overall business operations.

Impact: PromptaSynth allows businesses worldwide to create high-quality e-commerce content, understand market trends, and retain ownership of their data in a decentralized way.

Web3 Benefits:

Decentralized business performance data storage

Tokenized incentives for contributors

Open-source AI modules reusable by the Web3 community

2️⃣ Ecosystem Fit

Polkadot/Web3 Integration:

Stores business performance data on Substrate/Polkadot-compatible chains

Token reward system for contributors

Target Audience: Online businesses, e-commerce sellers, content creators worldwide

Why Now: Convergence of AI, e-commerce, and Web3 offers a unique opportunity to create a decentralized business optimization platform.
